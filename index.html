ABOUT GREENLINK

Technology Meets Climate Action

GreenLink provides a simple way for communities and young people to identify environmental challenges, organize solutions and track their impact.

📍

Report

Report environmental problems with a description, photo and location.

🤝

Connect

Connect climate challenges with young people ready to take action.

🌱

Act

Organize tree planting, clean-ups and other community climate activities.

📊

Measure

Record activities and demonstrate measurable environmental impact.
